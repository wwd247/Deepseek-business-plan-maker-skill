# Business Plan — Template Structure

Follow this section by section. Where a section poses instructional questions,
answer them in prose using the user's actual business details.

## 1. Executive Summary

One-page elevator pitch covering the whole plan. For a bank loan, state how
much is being borrowed, what for, and how it will be repaid.

*(Written last, by synthesis.)*

## 2. Company Description

### 2.1 Mission Statement
Purpose of the business, in the present tense. Original, with a USP, staff
connection, guiding principles. Short enough to fit on a t-shirt.

### 2.2 Vision Statement
What the company wants to achieve in the future.

### 2.3 Company Overview
A couple of paragraphs explaining who the company is and what it does.

### 2.4 Legal Structure
Limited Company, guaranteed by shares, or Partnership? Note the major
shareholder.

### 2.5 Ownership
Current ownership structure and details on major shareholders.

### 2.6 Locations
Registered address approach (e.g. virtual office until scale warrants premises).

### 2.7 Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
What differentiates the company from competitors.

### 2.8 Goals and Objectives
- **Goals**: Long-term, intangible (e.g. build a going concern).
- **Objectives**: Short-term, tangible milestones.

### 2.9 Business Philosophy
Tonality portraying honesty, integrity, long-term partnerships.

## 3. Market Analysis

### 3.1 Industry Overview
Growth trends, market size, projections.

### 3.2 Target Market
Define customers; segment by region, size, niche.

### 3.3 Market Demand
Demand for the products and why the business is well-positioned.

### 3.4 Competitive Landscape
Key competitors; comparison on market share, price, quality, innovation.

### 3.5 Regulatory Environment
Laws/regulations affecting the industry and jurisdiction(s).

## 4. Organization and Management

### 4.1 Management Team
Ideal structure as the company grows; profiles of key executives.

### 4.2 Board of Directors
If planning an IPO, outline existing or planned board.

### 4.3 Organizational Structure
Hierarchy, departments, key personnel.

### 4.4 Advisors and Partners
Advisors, consultants, partnerships.

## 5. Products or Services

### 5.1 Description
Detailed product/service offerings.

### 5.2 Research and Development
Ongoing or planned development.

### 5.3 Supply Chain
Sourcing and distribution; key suppliers, production, logistics.

## 6. Marketing and Sales Strategy

### 6.1 Branding and Niching Down
Market positioning (luxury, budget, mid-tier).

### 6.2 Routes to Market
Strategies to attract clients.

### 6.3 Sales Strategy
How products are sold and distributed.

### 6.4 Pricing Strategy
Pricing per revenue stream; comparison to competitors.

### 6.5 Marketing Campaigns
Online and offline campaigns; brand awareness.

## 7. Operational Plan

### 7.1 Operations Strategy
Day-to-day operations: efficiency, quality control, scalability.

### 7.2 Supply Chain Management
Raw materials, inventory, supplier relationships.

### 7.3 Technological Infrastructure
Specialised technology or software.

## 8. Financial Plan

### 8.1 Historical Financial Data
Income statements, balance sheets, cash flow (3–5 years).

### 8.2 Revenue Model
How the business makes money.

### 8.3 Profitability Projections
Projected revenue and profit over 3–5 years.
**IPO language** (include only if pursuing outside investment/IPO): highlight
growth potential after the IPO.

### 8.4 Funding Requirements
Capital needed and its use.

### 8.5 Break-Even Analysis
When the company reaches profitability.
**IPO language** (include only if pursuing outside investment/IPO): consider
additional IPO-related costs.

### 8.6 Risk Factors
Potential risks and mitigation.
